Pulse check

--beats per minute

  1. Sit and rest for 2 minutes first. Mornings, before getting up, give the truest resting reading.
  2. Rest your fingertip lightly over the back camera, and the flash if your phone has one. Pressing hard squeezes the blood out and hides your pulse.
  3. Keep your hand still and breathe normally for 30 seconds.
  4. On iPhone, websites can't switch on the flash, so measure in a bright room or near a lamp.
